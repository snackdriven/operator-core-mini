## Key synthesis claims and where they came from

### “Backpack is the practical center”

This synthesis came primarily from the live `backpack.json` structure and from the clarification that Chronicle was deprecated in practice.

### “The system needs Backpack, Doctrine, and Hoard”

This model was synthesized from:
- Backpack’s active carry-state behavior,
- narrator’s stable-facts / adaptive-framing philosophy,
- and the desire to preserve a deeper archive without forcing it into current carry-state.

### “Narrator is a renderer, not the center”

This comes from the narrator design documents and runtime files, especially the idea that narrators interpret but do not replace or rewrite the underlying system truth.

### “One truth layer, many renderers”

This was synthesized by comparing work dashboards, Backpack, narrator systems, life-state tools, and Claude continuity ideas across the repo constellation.
