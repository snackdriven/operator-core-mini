# Design Principles

## Local-first and inspectable

Important state should live in visible files, logs, manifests, vaults, or other locally owned artifacts rather than disappearing into opaque cloud memory.

## Ambient, not demanding

The system works best when it keeps the right information near the edge of awareness through HUDs, dashboards, dailies, sidebars, and statuslines rather than requiring constant active reconstruction.

## Stable facts, adaptive framing

Truth should remain stable even when the framing changes to match mood, energy, urgency, or role needs.

## Support over surveillance

The goal is accompaniment, continuity, and reduced reconstruction cost, not punishment or gamified control.

## Carry and keep

The system should support both a curated active carry-state and a larger deep archive. Backpack protects against overload; Hoard protects against loss.

## One truth layer, many renderers

The same underlying truth should be renderable as dashboard, narrator, daily brief, statusline, or assistant bootstrap depending on context.

## Editable by hand

The system should remain patchable by direct file edits when needed. Manual legibility and recovery matter more than elegance hidden behind tooling.
