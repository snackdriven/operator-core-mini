# Design Principles

## Local-first and inspectable

Important state should live in visible files, logs, manifests, vaults, or other locally owned artifacts rather than disappearing into opaque cloud memory.[cite:1][code_file:210][web:105]

## Ambient, not demanding

The system works best when it keeps the right information near the edge of awareness through HUDs, dashboards, dailies, sidebars, and statuslines rather than requiring constant active reconstruction.[cite:2][code_file:210][web:4]

## Stable facts, adaptive framing

Truth should remain stable even when the framing changes to match mood, energy, urgency, or role needs.[code_file:213]

## Support over surveillance

The goal is accompaniment, continuity, and reduced reconstruction cost, not punishment or gamified control.[code_file:214][code_file:209][cite:37]

## Carry and keep

The system should support both a curated active carry-state and a larger deep archive. Backpack protects against overload; Hoard protects against loss.[cite:1][web:105]

## One truth layer, many renderers

The same underlying truth should be renderable as dashboard, narrator, daily brief, statusline, or assistant bootstrap depending on context.[cite:1][cite:2][code_file:213]

## Editable by hand

The system should remain patchable by direct file edits when needed. Manual legibility and recovery matter more than elegance hidden behind tooling.[cite:1]
